About GoalProof

GoalProof is a small, independent project built on one idea: football predictions should be checkable. Every day's picks are sealed before kick-off and every result stays on the record, including the bad weeks.

We show probabilities, not promises. There are no ads, no betting links and no paid placements. GoalProof is not affiliated with any betting operator, league or club.

Questions, corrections or problems: support@plainwharf.com. We reply within two working days.
